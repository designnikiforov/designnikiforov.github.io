How do I restore my Pro subscription on a new device?

Carvin has no account system of its own — your subscription is tied to your Apple ID, not to a login. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you subscribed with, open Carvin, and tap Restore Purchases on the Pro screen (Settings → Carvin Pro).

My vehicles and records aren’t showing up on my other device

Carvin syncs through your own private iCloud account, not through a server of ours. Check that:

  • Both devices are signed in with the same Apple ID
  • iCloud is turned on for Carvin (Settings app → your name → iCloud)
  • Both devices have an internet connection

Sync can take a minute or two after you make a change.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed and managed entirely by Apple, not by Carvin. On your iPhone: Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Carvin, or from inside the app: Settings → Carvin Pro → Manage Subscription.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting a vehicle in Carvin permanently deletes everything linked to it — records, documents, photos — and this can’t be undone. To remove everything at once, delete the app from your device; you can also clear its iCloud data from Settings app → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Can I change the currency, distance units, or language?

Yes — all three are in the app’s own Settings and can be changed at any time without losing any data.
