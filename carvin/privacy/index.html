Carvin Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. Summary

Carvin has no backend server of its own. Everything you enter — vehicles, maintenance records, expenses, documents, receipt photos, mileage history — is stored using Apple’s CloudKit, inside your own private iCloud account. We do not host, see, or have access to this data ourselves. The only data that leaves your device for a purpose other than your own iCloud sync is small, anonymous usage analytics, described in Section 3.

2. Data stored via iCloud (not on our servers)

When you use Carvin, the following is saved locally on your device and synced through your private iCloud account so it’s available across your own devices:

  • Vehicles you add (make, model, year, mileage)
  • Maintenance, repair, and expense records, including amounts and categories
  • Documents you log (insurance, inspection, tax, fines) and their dates
  • Receipt photos you attach to records
  • App settings (currency, measurement units, theme)

This data is protected by your Apple ID and Apple’s iCloud security. Carvin has no server-side database and cannot access this data independent of your device.

Identity: Carvin does not have its own account system. There is no sign-up, no password, and Carvin never sees your Apple ID, email, or iCloud credentials — your identity is handled entirely by Apple.

3. Analytics

Carvin uses PostHog to understand basic, anonymous usage patterns — for example, how often the app is opened — so we can improve it. Specifically:

  • Analytics are anonymous: Carvin never sends your name, email, or any other personal identifier to PostHog.
  • We do not record your screen, log individual taps, or track your precise location.
  • Standard technical information (such as device type, OS version, and IP address at the moment of connection) is received by PostHog as part of normal network communication, the same as with any online service — this is used only for basic, aggregate technical context, never to identify you personally.
  • PostHog processes this data on servers located in the United States.

You cannot be identified from this data, and it is never linked to the vehicle or financial information described in Section 2.

4. Notifications

Carvin sends reminders (for example, about upcoming document expiry or mileage-based maintenance) using local, on-device notifications. These are scheduled directly on your phone and are never sent through a remote server.

5. Purchases

Carvin Pro subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. Carvin never receives or stores your payment card details — we only receive confirmation of your subscription status from Apple.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers. Data is only handled by:

  • Apple — iCloud (CloudKit) storage and sync, and payment processing via the App Store
  • PostHog — anonymous usage analytics, as described in Section 3

7. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays in your own iCloud account for as long as you keep it in the app. Deleting a vehicle or record in Carvin deletes it (and anything linked to it) permanently — this cannot be undone. You can also remove all app data at any time by deleting the app and its iCloud data from your device’s Settings.

8. Children’s privacy

Carvin is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country).

9. Your rights

If you are in the European Union, United Kingdom, or another region with data protection laws, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Because your Carvin data lives in your own iCloud account, you already have full, direct control over it inside the app and your device’s iCloud settings. For anything else, or for questions about the anonymous analytics described in Section 3, contact us using the details below.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of Carvin after a change means you accept the updated policy.
